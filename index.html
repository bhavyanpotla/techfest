Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Who can participate in the Tech Fest?

    The event is open to all university students, high school seniors, and tech enthusiasts. Registrations are required to secure your spot.

  2. Is there a registration fee?

    No, the event is completely free of charge. This includes access to all workshops, keynote speeches, and coding hackathons.
